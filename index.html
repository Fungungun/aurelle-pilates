Foundations 66 节入门课

From your first class to a full workout, in six sessions.6 节课，从第一次上器械，到完整的一节课。

Each session adds one new layer to what you already know, so nothing feels like a leap.每节课只在你已经会的基础上加一层新内容，不会突然变难。

Session 1第 1 节

Meet the reformer认识器械

Getting on and off safely, finding neutral spine, breathing, and your first footwork series.安全上下器械，找到中立位，学会呼吸，完成第一组脚部系列。

Session 2第 2 节

Legs and straps单腿与脚套

Single-leg work, your first time in the straps, and preparing for the Hundred.单腿练习，第一次使用脚套，为 Hundred 做准备。

Session 3第 3 节

The Hundred and Elephant完整 Hundred 与大象

The full Hundred, leg circles, and your first time standing on the moving carriage.完整的 Hundred、腿部画圈，第一次站上会滑动的车架。

Session 4第 4 节

Kneeling and the long box跪姿与长箱

A second layer of core work, kneeling for the first time, and back extension on the box.第二层核心训练，第一次跪姿练习，在长箱上做背部伸展。

Session 5第 5 节

Finding flow找到流畅感

Linking exercises with fewer pauses, plus new stretches and preparations.动作之间衔接更流畅、停顿更少，加入新的拉伸和进阶准备。

Session 6第 6 节

Graduation毕业课

A complete 55-minute class from start to finish, and a re-assessment to see how far you've come.从头到尾完成一节 55 分钟的完整课程，并复评，看看自己进步了多少。

Most people complete the path in 4 to 8 weeks, at one or two sessions a week.每周上 1–2 节，大多数人 4–8 周完成。

Questions常见问题

Good to know.上课前你可能想知道的

I've never done Pilates. Is that OK?我从来没练过普拉提，可以吗？

Yes. The Foundations path is designed for complete beginners, and we start from how to get on the reformer.完全可以。6 节入门课就是为零基础设计的，从怎么上器械开始教。

Do I need to be fit or flexible?需要有体能或柔韧性基础吗？

No. Springs are set for you, and every exercise has an easier option. Strength and flexibility are what you build here.不需要。弹簧阻力会按你的情况调整，每个动作都有简化版本。力量和柔韧性正是你要在这里练出来的。

I have an injury, I'm pregnant, or I've had recent surgery.我有伤病、正在怀孕或最近做过手术，可以上吗？

Please mention it when you book. Some conditions need clearance from your GP or physio first, and some are better looked after by a clinical Pilates practitioner. I'll always be honest about which is right for you.请在预约时告诉我。有些情况需要先得到医生或物理治疗师的许可，有些更适合去临床普拉提（康复）机构。我会如实告诉你哪种更适合。

What should I wear and bring?要穿什么、带什么？

Fitted, comfortable activewear, grip socks and a water bottle. Loose tops can catch on the equipment.贴身舒适的运动服、防滑袜和一瓶水。太宽松的上衣容易卡到器械。

What is your cancellation policy?取消和改期怎么算？

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Later cancellations are charged as a session, but your first one is on me.请至少提前 24 小时取消或改期。不足 24 小时的取消按一节课计算，第一次可以免除。

How do I pay?怎么付款？

By PayID, bank transfer or card, before or at your session.课前或上课时，用 PayID、银行转账或刷卡付款。
